For whoever presents

Say

  • "Past years", "good years", "rain covered 64% of what it needed".
  • Seven places with a weather download and a measured soil sample.
  • It says no: cabbage, lettuce and potato are ruled out in the warm lowlands.
  • Every crop number names its source, and the rejected source is shown too.

Do not say

  • "Accuracy", "success rate" or "probability". Nothing has been checked against a harvest.
  • "Covers the Philippines".
  • That mungbean is drought-proof. At Muñoz no crop had a good year in a dry year.
  • That the rainfall trend is climate change. It is a trend in one NASA record.
