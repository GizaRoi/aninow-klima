# Crop photo credits

The results cards use these 29 photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Author and license metadata were checked against the original Commons file records on 15 November 2026, and a contact sheet was visually reviewed for crop identity and framing.

Each local image is a 720 × 360 WebP derivative. Changes: resizing, cropping where appropriate, WebP compression, and white padding for the onion, sweet pepper, and cucumber photographs. No generated imagery is used. Each photograph and its derivative remain under the license shown below; those licenses do not change the license of the application code.

These are representative photographs of the named crops, not photographs of a particular farmer’s field or a claim about the model’s evaluated cultivar. Sweet corn uses a source explicitly identifying the “Tasty sweet” cultivar; grain maize uses mature dried cobs. Yardlong bean and cowpea use separate photographs. The squash photo specifically shows Philippine kalabasa. No crop IDs share the same image.

The UI reads attribution from [crop-photos.json](../apps/web/lib/crop-photos.json). Keep its source and license links with the photographs when reusing these assets.
